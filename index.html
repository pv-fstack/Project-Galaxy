Chapter 01

🌌 What is a Galaxy?

A galaxy is a colossal collection of gas, dust, and billions of stars all held together by gravity. Think of it like a giant city of stars floating in the darkness of space!

Our own galaxy is called the Milky Way. It holds over 200 billion stars — including our very own Sun! 🌞

Scientists estimate there are 2 trillion galaxies in the observable universe. That's more than the number of grains of sand on all of Earth's beaches combined!

🤯 Mind-Bender: If you could travel at the speed of light — that's 299,792 km every single second — it would still take 100,000 years to cross the Milky Way from one side to the other!

Chapter 03

🔭 Types of Galaxies

Not all galaxies look the same! Scientists classify them into three main shapes based on how they appear from Earth.

🌀

Spiral Galaxies

These look like enormous pinwheels with curved arms sweeping out from a bright center. Our own Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy! About 60% of all known galaxies are spirals.

🥚

Elliptical Galaxies

Shaped like stretched spheres or ovals — some look like footballs! They are the largest type of galaxy and are packed with old, reddish stars with very little dust or new star formation.

✨

Irregular Galaxies

These have no recognizable shape at all — completely chaotic and jumbled up! They often form when two galaxies collide with each other and their gravity rips everything apart.

Chapter 05

🤩 Mind-Blowing Space Facts

The universe is full of jaw-dropping surprises. Here are six that will make your brain spin!

01

There's no sound in space. Sound waves need air to travel. Space is a near-perfect vacuum — totally silent!

02

A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus. It spins so slowly that it takes longer to rotate once than to orbit the Sun!

03

Neutron stars are incredibly dense — a single teaspoon of neutron star material would weigh around 10 billion tonnes!

04

The Sun makes up 99.86% of all the mass in our entire Solar System. Everything else — planets, moons, asteroids — is just 0.14%!

05

Black holes are regions where gravity is so powerful that nothing — not even light — can ever escape once it crosses the event horizon.

06

Moon footprints left by Apollo astronauts will stay there for millions of years — there's no wind, weather, or water to wash them away!
